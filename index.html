How it works

  1. 01

    Align

    Product-specific PCF methods aligned with ISO 14067 and the TfS guideline.

  2. 02

    Calculate

    Consistent, comparable product carbon intensity in t CO₂e per tonne.

  3. 03

    Benchmark

    Compare with peers against GOOD BETTER BEST bands.

  4. 04

    Communicate

    Verified results and the BEST carbon label send a clear market signal.

  5. 05

    Improve

    Turn benchmark results into actionable improvement on energy, feedstock and process.
