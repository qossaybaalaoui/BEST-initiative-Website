Benchmarking & Evaluation for Sustainability and Transparency

Turning product carbon performance into business value

BEST is a product carbon performance evaluation and benchmarking system. It builds on international standards and turns product carbon footprint (PCF) data into comparable performance, clear market signals and practical improvement.

Explore benchmarks Read the methods

How it works

Align

Product-specific PCF methods aligned with ISO 14067 and the TfS guideline.

Calculate

Consistent, comparable product carbon intensity in t CO₂e per tonne.

Benchmark

Compare with peers against GOOD BETTER BEST bands.

Communicate

Verified results and the BEST carbon label send a clear market signal.

Improve

Turn benchmark results into actionable improvement on energy, feedstock and process.
