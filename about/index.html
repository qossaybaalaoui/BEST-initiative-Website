About BEST

BEST — Benchmarking & Evaluation for Sustainability and Transparency — makes product carbon performance credible, comparable and economically meaningful.

What BEST is

An integrated system to evaluate and benchmark the carbon performance of products — built on international standards, with the product-specific rules, benchmarks and verification they leave open.

Producers see where they stand and where to improve. Buyers and investors get results they can compare. Governments get a credible basis for linking verified performance to incentives.

Who develops BEST

Researchers at the University of California, Berkeley, in collaboration with international stakeholders from industry and academia.

Methods are reviewed by international experts before publication.

Why it matters

Footprints are only useful if they can be compared

General standards leave key choices open — boundary, co-product allocation, electricity, carbon capture, data quality. Results can't be compared, and strong performers can't prove it.

Product-specific methods fix those choices; a shared factor library and calculator apply them; benchmarks place every result in a BEST, BETTER or GOOD band.

How it works

From measurement to value

Align

Each product gets its own BEST method, built on ISO 14067 and the TfS PCF Guideline, so boundaries, allocation and data rules are fixed in advance.

Calculate

Producers calculate the cradle-to-gate footprint per tonne with the BEST calculator and a shared factor library, plus a data quality rating.

Benchmark

The result is placed against peers in a BEST, BETTER or GOOD band derived from industry evidence.

Communicate

Verified results can carry the BEST label — a clear, comparable signal to customers and supply chains.

Improve

The gap to the next tier shows where energy, feedstock, utilities or process changes matter most.

Create value

Better performance becomes visible to buyers, investors and governments — opening market advantages and performance-linked incentives.

Principles

Six rules every BEST method follows

They apply to every product and sector, so results stay comparable as BEST grows.

Location-based results are mandatory; market-based results are shown separately where contracts are used.

Captured CO₂ is credited only with complete evidence of capture and permanent storage.

A clear data hierarchy and a Data Quality Rating with Low, Medium or High uncertainty.

Biogenic carbon is tracked and reported separately — never netted as a credit.

Method development

Open, reviewed, versioned

Draft

The research team drafts the method with industry input and worked examples.

Expert review

International experts from industry, academia, research and certification review the draft.

Revision

Comments are consolidated and addressed, and the calculator is updated to match.

Approval

The method and its benchmark thresholds are approved and published with a version number.

Value

Why companies take part

See how products compare with peers and focus on energy, feedstocks, utilities and processes that also cut cost.

Make stronger performance visible, meet product-carbon requirements and stand out in domestic and export markets.

Build evidence for buyer preference and performance-linked benefits: tax relief, rebates, grants, financing or procurement.

Two signals that create value

Benchmarking and verified labels let procurement programmes, investors and supply chains reward better performers.

Verified improvement gives governments a sound basis for incentives — as the UK's Climate Change Agreements already do for energy-intensive industry.
