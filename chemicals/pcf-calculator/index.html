How it works

Enter one year of plant data step by step — feedstocks, energy, utilities, transport and waste. The calculator applies the BEST Chemicals method and factor library, then shows your footprint per tonne, your BEST / BETTER / GOOD tier, where emissions come from and your data quality rating. Calculations run in your browser: your plant data stays on your computer.
