What makes this method specific

Propylene, C4s and other co-products share the cracker's emissions. Ethylene's share is set by mass; economic allocation only where values differ by more than 5:1.

Burning coke off cracking furnaces is counted as its own emission source, not hidden in fuel use.

Captured CO₂ is deducted only with full evidence of capture and storage — the result before capture is always shown too.

Results are reported by route and feedstock, so each plant is compared on the same basis.

What makes this method specific

Ethylene is the main input. Its own footprint — ideally the supplier's verified BEST ethylene result — flows into the polyethylene result.

Catalysts and initiators are never cut off: tiny in mass, but their upstream factors can be high.

Density and melt flow rate are reported for each grade but never multiply the footprint.

Bio-ethylene is tracked stream by stream; biogenic carbon is reported separately and never netted.

Off-spec resin and wax share emissions only when sold; everything else is classified before allocation.
