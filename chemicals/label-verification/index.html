What the label shows

  1. The product and its declared unit — always 1 tonne at the factory gate.
  2. The footprint in t CO₂e per tonne, fossil and location-based. Biogenic carbon and carbon capture are disclosed separately.
  3. The tier map — where the product sits against BEST, BETTER and GOOD thresholds for the same product.
  4. Data quality — the uncertainty class from the data quality rating.
  5. Verification — the label is issued only after independent third-party verification.
Performance tiers

What each tier means

Thresholds are set per product from comparable industry evidence and published with their approval status. Values still in draft are labelled as such.

BEST

At or below the BEST threshold — the top band among comparable plants.

BETTER

Above BEST, at or below the BETTER threshold — better than the typical plant.

GOOD

Above BETTER, at or below the GOOD threshold — within the range of comparable plants.

ABOVE RANGE

Above the GOOD threshold — the improvement gap shows where to act first.

From result to label

How a result becomes a verified label

Calculate

The producer calculates the footprint with the BEST calculator for one year of plant data.

Resolve checks

No blocking issue or provisional factor may remain, every warning is explained and the data quality assessment is complete.

Verify

An independent third party checks the data, boundary, allocation, balances, factors and data quality.

Issue the label

With the verification statement in hand, the BEST label and tier can be used in public claims and procurement.

Self-assessment is not assurance. A producer can use its own result to find improvements, but public claims, procurement, tier disclosure and the BEST label always require independent third-party verification.
For verifiers

What verifiers check

The minimum scope of a BEST verification. Select an item for detail.

Primary data are complete, accurate and representative of the reporting year.

The cradle-to-gate boundary matches the reported result and the BEST method.

The co-product rule was applied and justified; factors sum to 1; wastes and co-products are classified correctly.

Carbon and energy close — feedstock, products, co-products, recycle and captured carbon, fossil and biogenic separately.

The data hierarchy was followed; database name, version, geography and technology are disclosed.

Market-based claims rest on owned, tracked and retired instruments that match in time and place.

The data quality rating, primary data share and uncertainty class are calculated correctly.

Exclusions respect the thresholds and no known high-impact flow is left out.

Only the permanently stored or verifiably used CO₂ is deducted, with monitoring and claim ownership documented.

Calculator checks

Four result statuses

The BEST calculator flags issues as you go, so results arrive at verification clean.

BLOCK

Must be fixed — no result is produced.

PROVISIONAL

A factor is still provisional — replace it before reporting.

WARN

Allowed, but must be explained in the report.

INFO

A disclosure for transparency — no action needed.

Verification statement

Every statement names

  • The verifier
  • The assurance level — limited or reasonable
  • The standard applied
  • The scope covered
  • The completion date
