Glossary

Key terms in plain words

Product carbon footprint (PCF)
Greenhouse-gas emissions of a product per declared unit, in t CO₂e per tonne, following ISO 14067.
Cradle-to-gate
From raw-material extraction to the factory gate. Use, end-of-life and downstream processing are outside the boundary.
Declared unit
The reference amount a footprint refers to — in BEST Chemicals, 1 tonne of specification-compliant product at the factory gate.
CO₂e and GWP-100
Different greenhouse gases converted to the warming effect of CO₂ over 100 years, using IPCC AR6 values.
Co-product
A second output of the same process that is sold, such as propylene from a steam cracker. It shares the process emissions.
Allocation
The rule for sharing emissions between a product and its sold co-products: by mass by default, by economic value when values differ by more than 5:1.
Cut-off
Leaving out a very small input — below 1% of the footprint — while still covering at least 97% of mass and energy. Never applied to catalysts.
Carbon residual
Carbon that enters the process but is not found in any measured output. BEST counts it as emitted CO₂, to stay conservative.
Location-based electricity
Electricity counted with the average delivered factor of the local grid. Mandatory in BEST.
Market-based electricity
Electricity counted with contractual instruments (renewable contracts, certificates) and the residual mix. Reported separately.
Supplier-specific PCF
A supplier's own verified footprint for the material it delivers — the preferred data for any purchased input.
Primary data
Data measured at the site for the reporting period, as opposed to database or default values.
Primary data share (PDS)
The share of emissions based on measured activity data and site- or supplier-specific factors.
Data quality rating (DQR)
A score from 1 (best) to 5 for each input, weighted by its share of emissions into one overall rating.
Uncertainty class
Low (rating up to 1.5), Medium (up to 3.0) or High (above 3.0), derived from the data quality rating.
Biogenic carbon
Carbon from biomass, such as bio-ethylene. Tracked and reported separately from fossil carbon; never netted as a credit.
Carbon capture (CCUS)
Capturing CO₂ for storage or use. Only permanently stored or verifiably used CO₂ may be deducted.
Offset
A credit from an external project. Never deducted from a BEST footprint; reported separately if at all.
Benchmark tier
BEST, BETTER, GOOD or Above range — where a footprint sits against thresholds for comparable plants.
Threshold
The value that separates two tiers for a given product. Published with its approval status.
Third-party verification
An independent check of a footprint, at limited or reasonable assurance, required for public claims and the BEST label.
BLOCK · PROVISIONAL · WARN · INFO
Calculator statuses: must fix / provisional factor to replace / allowed with explanation / disclosure only.
Steam cracking
Breaking down ethane, naphtha or other hydrocarbons with steam at high temperature to make ethylene and co-products.
Methanol-to-olefins (MTO)
Making ethylene and propylene from methanol, which itself comes from coal or natural gas.
LDPE · LLDPE · HDPE
Low-density, linear low-density and high-density polyethylene — three resin families made by different processes.
TfS PCF Guideline
The chemical industry's guideline for product carbon footprints (Together for Sustainability), on which BEST builds.
